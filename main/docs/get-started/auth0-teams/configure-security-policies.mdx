Security policies allow [team owners](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/team-member-management) to configure and implement authentication rules that adhere to your organization's IT security policies for access to infrastructure systems or applications.

## Available Enterprise IdP Connections

Auth0 Teams allows you to connect to your <Tooltip tip="Identity Provider (IdP): Service that stores and manages digital identities." cta="View Glossary" href="/docs/glossary?term=identity+provider">identity provider</Tooltip> (IdP) to provide single-sign on (<Tooltip tip="Identity Provider (IdP): Service that stores and manages digital identities." cta="View Glossary" href="/docs/glossary?term=SSO">SSO</Tooltip>) for team members.

### Add SSO connection

You can configure an SSO connection in the Auth0 Teams Dashboard.

1. Go to [**Security Policies**](https://accounts.auth0.com/teams/#/security-policies).
2. Select **Connections**.
3. Select **Add Connection**.
4. Select an identity provider.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/1.png" alt="Add Connection - Select your Identity Provider" />
</Frame>

5. Add the connection and configure the required details.
6. To ensure the connection appears on the login page, select **Show as Button**.

Once set up, the connection appears on the **Connections** page. Use the toggle to enable or disable the connection.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/2.png" alt="Connections page with enable/disable toggle" />
</Frame>

### Domain Verification

To use Home Realm Discovery, you must first verify your domain.

1. Go to [**Security Policies**](https://accounts.auth0.com/teams/#/security-policies) and select **Domain Verification**.
2. Select **Add Domain**.
3. Enter the domain URL.
4. Add the provided TXT record to your DNS settings.
5. Select **Verify** to initiate domain verification.

<Callout icon="file-lines" color="#0EA5E9" iconType="regular">

**Verification Status: Pending** — Your domain is pending verification. Select **Verify** to refresh the status, or check back later. DNS changes may take up to 48 hours, but typically process faster.

</Callout>

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/3.png" alt="Domains page showing a verified domain" />
</Frame>

You can also delete a domain if needed.

<Callout icon="file-lines" color="#0EA5E9" iconType="regular">

To use Home Realm Discovery, your domains must be verified.

</Callout>

### Configure home-realm discovery (HRD)

If you enable HRD, Auth0 recognizes the domain of the email address a team member enters and directs them to the associated SSO connection.

<Callout icon="triangle-exclamation" color="#F59E0B" iconType="solid">

Before you enable HRD, make sure your team members are prepared!

- If you're using Team Member Management and JIT provisioning, notify your team members of the new behavior.
- If you're managing team membership manually, ensure all team members have an account on the associated SSO connection and they know how to log in.

</Callout>

There are two ways to configure HRD:

**Via Domain Verification:**

1. Go to **Domain Verification**.
2. Select a verified domain.
3. Open the three-dot menu and select **Configure**.
4. From the list of connections, toggle **ON** the connection to associate with the domain.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/4.png" alt="Configure HRD modal - select connection to associate with domain" />
</Frame>

**Via Add or Edit Connection:**

1. Go to **Connections** and open an existing or new connection.
2. Select the **Home Realm Discovery** tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/6.png" alt="Edit connection page showing the Home Realm Discovery tab" />
</Frame>

3. Verified domains appear in the list. Toggle **ON** the domain you want to associate with the connection.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/7.png" alt="Home Realm Discovery tab showing verified domains with toggle" />
</Frame>

### Edit Connections

With Auth0 Teams SSO, you can update the following connection metadata after setup:

* **Metadata**
* **Secret**
* **Certificate**
* **Associated Domains**

To edit a connection:

1. Go to **Connections** and select **Edit Connection** for the connection you want to update.
2. Navigate to the setting you want to update and make your changes.

### Configure just-in-time (JIT) provisioning

JIT provisioning enables Auth0 to automatically create an account for team members who log in through an SSO connection.

<Callout icon="file-lines" color="#0EA5E9" iconType="regular">

You must enable [Tenant Member Management](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/tenant-member-management) to configure JIT provisioning.

</Callout>

1. Go to **Connections** and select **Add Connection** or open an existing connection.
2. Navigate to the bottom of the page and enable **Assign Membership on Login**.

## Enforce Single Sign On

Auth0 Teams allows you to require team members to log in through one of your **Available Enterprise IdP Connections**.

<Callout icon="triangle-exclamation" color="#F59E0B" iconType="solid">

Before turning on Enforce SSO:

- Ensure you have at least one active connection configured and at least one Team Owner role assigned to a member from that connection. Failing to do so may result in account lockout.
- You must be logged in through an SSO connection and have the **Team Owner** role. If you are not, but you have a connection configured, you can [invite yourself to your Auth0 Team](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/team-member-management) on that connection.
- Auth0 Teams does not allow disabling the last active connection in your team when Enforce SSO is turned on, to prevent accidental account lockouts.

</Callout>

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/5.png" alt="Enforce Single Sign On toggle" />
</Frame>

### Use JIT provisioning

This method allows team members to log in to the SSO connection immediately after it's enabled, and instructs Auth0 to automatically create an account for them after the first time they log in successfully.

1. Enable [Tenant Member Management](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/tenant-member-management).
2. Enable the **JIT Membership** toggle for the SSO connection.
3. Go to the Teams Dashboard’s Settings page and note your team's permalink value.
4. Instruct all team members to log out of the Auth0 Teams Dashboard and log in via the SSO connection. The URL structure for the team members to follow is `https://accounts.auth0.com/teams/{team-permalink}`.
5. Auth0 automatically creates a new account (on the SSO connection) for each team member.
6. Assign each team member's new account the same [team role](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/team-member-management) as their old account.
7. (Optional) Delete each team member's old account.

### Manage team and tenant membership manually

This method allows you to manage team members separately from tenant members.

For team members, [send a new invitation from the Teams Dashboard](/docs/get-started/auth0-teams/team-member-management) and instruct them to accept the invitation using the SSO connection.

For tenant members, [send a new invitation from the Auth0 Dashboard](/docs/get-started/manage-dashboard-access/add-dashboard-users) from each tenant they're a member of and instruct them to accept the invitation using the SSO connection.

After accepting the invitation, Private Cloud Enterprise customers that enforce SSO and/or use Tenant Member Management can click the **Continue with Auth0 Teams** login button in their Private Cloud <Tooltip tip="" cta="View Glossary" href="/docs/glossary?term=Auth0+Dashboard">Auth0 Dashboard</Tooltip>.

## Access Your Tenant Dashboard

<Callout icon="file-lines" color="#0EA5E9" iconType="regular">

This capability is available for Public Cloud customers only.

</Callout>

Once SSO is configured, you can navigate directly to your tenant's Auth0 Dashboard from the Teams Dashboard.

1. Go to **Tenants** in the Teams Dashboard. Your tenants are listed with their name, environment, and region.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/8.png" alt="Tenants page showing tenant list with clickable tenant names" />
</Frame>

2. Select the tenant name — it appears as a hyperlink in the tenant list.
3. You are taken to the Auth0 Dashboard for that tenant.

<Frame>
  <img src="/docs/images/auth0-teams/9.png" alt="Auth0 Dashboard after navigating from the Teams Dashboard" />
</Frame>
