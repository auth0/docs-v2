The Auth0 Identity Conversion Suite bundles two Auth0 features that help you turn your login experience into a growth lever rather than a fixed checkpoint:

- [**Anonymous Sessions**](/docs/manage-users/sessions/anonymous-sessions): Track and manage users from their very first interaction, before they authenticate, and carry that activity into their authenticated profile when they sign up or log in.
- [**Experiment Center**](/docs/customize/experiment-center): Run A/B tests and gradual rollouts on your authentication flows, so you can measure the impact of changes like new sign-up fields, passkeys, or MFA rules before shipping them to all your users.

## Why use them together

Anonymous Sessions and Experiment Center solve complementary problems:

- Without Anonymous Sessions, you have no way to identify or carry forward pre-authentication activity, such as browsing, cart contents, or consent choices, often leading to abandoned carts and lost signups.
- Without Experiment Center, you can't test changes to sign-up and login flows, such as new authentication methods or security rules, against a slice of traffic first. You either limit innovation or risk an all-or-nothing rollout.

Used together, the two features answer different questions. Experiment Center tells you whether a change to your authentication flow worked. Anonymous Sessions tells you who it worked for, by giving you a pre-authentication segment, such as users who saved an item before signing up, to split your experiment results by. Without that segment, a real effect on a specific group of users can get diluted into a smaller, less convincing number once it's averaged across everyone.
